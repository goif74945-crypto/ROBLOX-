# HORRORFORGE 3D MODEL STYLE

## Core direction

Grounded dark survival-horror props and entities with readable silhouettes, functional construction and a consistent field-industrial material language.

## Mandatory rules

- models must communicate purpose and gameplay state through silhouette and construction
- avoid toy-like proportions and generic free-model appearance
- materials should support the environmental story: worn field gear, damp surfaces, damaged infrastructure and restrained industrial hardware
- collision and interaction volumes are separate from visual detail where appropriate
- hero models require a documented scale, pivot, attachment points and performance budget
- low-value decorative detail must not consume runtime budget without visible gameplay value

## Asset classes

Environment, evidence props, equipment, interactive devices, Entity E01, extraction equipment and UI-associated 3D assets.

No binary model/texture/audio asset is invented merely to make a folder look populated. Missing source assets remain explicitly missing until created and reviewed.
